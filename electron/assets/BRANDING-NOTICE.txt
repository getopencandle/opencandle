Recipe: scripts/generate-opencandle-icons.py (Python with Pillow)
Assets: opencandle.ico, opencandle-256.png, opencandle-logo-512.png, opencandle-mark-64.png,
        opencandle-favicon-32.png, opencandle-tray-16/20/24/32.png
The 16, 20 and 24 px sizes and the 64 px menu mark use a simplified drawing (flame and
green candle only) so the icon stays readable at small sizes.

The earlier pre-release bitmap and mark are not used and are not included.
